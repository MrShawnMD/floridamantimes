Florida Man Cams

Live, right now, somewhere in Florida: manatees piling into a power plant, a party on Duval Street, a rocket about to leave the planet. Pull up a chair.

● LIVE
Manatees · Orange City

🌊 Blue Spring Manatee Cam

Above-water and underwater cams in a crystal-clear spring where hundreds of manatees pile in to stay warm. It's a sea cow traffic jam, and it's the most relaxing thing on the internet.

Best time: Cold mornings, November to March

Watch live on Save the Manatee Club ↗
● LIVE
Manatees · Homosassa

🐟 Homosassa Springs Underwater Cam

An underwater view from the state park's observatory. Manatees float past the glass like they own the place, with a supporting cast of snook, mullet and the occasional show-off fish.

Best time: Daytime, winter for the most manatees

Watch live on Save the Manatee Club ↗
● LIVE
Manatees · Apollo Beach

⚡ Manatees at the Power Plant

Only in Florida: manatees spend winter in the warm water coming out of a power plant. Tampa Electric's Manatee Viewing Center cams even let you steer the camera yourself.

Best time: Winter, through April 15

Watch live on Tampa Electric ↗
● LIVE
Party · Key West

🍹 Sloppy Joe's Crowd & Stage Cams

The legendary bar at Duval and Greene, live. Watch the crowd, the band and whatever Duval Street decides to do tonight. Peak people-watching after 10 p.m.

Best time: Friday and Saturday nights

Watch live on Visit Florida Keys ↗
● LIVE
Sunset · Key West

🌅 Mallory Square Sunset Cam

Every evening, Key West gathers on the dock to clap for the sun going down. Fire jugglers and cats-doing-tricks not guaranteed on camera, but the sunset is.

Best time: Sunset, every day

Watch live on Visit Florida Keys ↗
● LIVE
Rockets · Titusville / Port Canaveral

🚀 Space Coast Launch Cam

A live view toward Kennedy Space Center. On launch days you can watch a rocket leave Florida, which is one of the few things that leaves Florida faster than a tourist in August.

Best time: Launch days (check the schedule)

Watch live on FOX 35 Live Cams ↗
● LIVE
Beach · Clearwater Beach

🏖️ Clearwater Beach Pier 60

White sand, the famous pier and a nightly sunset festival. Also excellent for spotting spring breakers, pelicans and that one guy with a metal detector.

Best time: Sunset, and spring break for chaos

Watch live on FOX 35 Live Cams ↗
● LIVE
Beach · Daytona Beach

🏁 Daytona Beach Cam

The beach where you can still drive your car on the sand. Bike Week and spring break turn this cam into a reality show.

Best time: Bike Week (March) and Biketoberfest

Watch live on FOX 35 Live Cams ↗
● LIVE
Underwater · Miami

🐠 Miami Coral Reef Underwater Cam

A camera on the sea floor off Miami. Tropical fish, swaying coral and the occasional curious visitor staring right into the lens.

Best time: Daytime

Watch live on SkylineWebcams ↗
