Weird Eats in Florida

Gator tail, frog legs, elk, swamp cabbage, and the time a Tampa taco shop sold lion. Where to eat the strangest things in the Sunshine State. Menus change, so call ahead.

The Yearling Restaurant

Restaurant · Hawthorne

Gator tailFrog legsVenisonQuail

Old Florida cracker cooking since 1952, named after the Marjorie Kinnan Rawlings novel. Gator starter, a frog legs dinner and venison, served under mounted wildlife.

Red Wing Restaurant

Restaurant · Groveland

Elk chopsGator tailFrog legsVenison sausage

Wild game is the star at this Lake County landmark, which also has a petting zoo. Yes, you can meet the animals and eat different animals in the same visit.

Festival food · LaBelle

Lion Chop at Spoto's Steak Joint

Restaurant · Dunedin

African lionRattlesnakeKangarooWild boar

A Dunedin steakhouse once advertised lion, rattlesnake, bison, elk and boar right on its marquee. A reviewer said the 14-ounce lion chop tasted a bit like ribs.

$35 Lion Tacos at Taco Fusion

Restaurant · Tampa

LionRattlesnakeKangarooOstrichShark

A Tampa taco shop's 'Safari Tacos' menu ran from shark to rattlesnake. Then it added $35 lion tacos, the internet roared, and the lion came off the menu.

Source: National Geographic ↗

The $45 Python Pizza

Pizzeria · Fort Myers

PythonAlligator sausageFrog legsSwamp cabbage

A Fort Myers pizzeria's 'Everglades Pizza' piled on python, gator sausage, frog legs and swamp cabbage. Diners said python ate like chewy, gamey chicken. The shop has since closed.
