The Florida Weed Guide

Where Florida's medical marijuana patients shop, how to get your card, and the rules that keep you out of our Weirdest Arrests page.

First things first

Florida is medical-only. Recreational marijuana fell short of the 60% it needed in 2024, and the 2026 version didn't make the ballot. To buy at a dispensary you need a state Medical Marijuana Use Registry card.

How to get a card

  1. 1

    See a qualified doctor

    Only doctors registered with the state can certify you. Your first visit has to be an in-person exam; renewals can often be done by video. Visits usually run about $150 to $250.

  2. 2

    Doctor adds you to the registry

    If you qualify, the doctor enters you into Florida's Medical Marijuana Use Registry.

  3. 3

    Apply and pay the state fee

    You finish the application online and pay the state's $75 card fee. You'll get an email you can use while the plastic card is in the mail.

  4. 4

    Renew on time

    Doctor certifications and the card both have to be renewed, and the state fee applies each time. Set a reminder.

Picking the good stuff

🧑‍🔬

Ask the budtender

Staff know what just came in and what patients keep coming back for. Tell them what you're after.

🏷️

Read the label

Florida products list THC and CBD content and a harvest or packaging date. Fresher flower is usually better.

💸

Watch the deals

Chains run daily and first-time patient discounts. Sign up for texts at the ones you like.

Rules that keep you off our front page

🚗

Don't drive high

A medical card is not a DUI pass. Florida's DUI law covers drugs too, and yes, that includes golf carts and lawn mowers.

🏖️

Not in public

Smoking medical marijuana in public places is not allowed. Keep it at home.

🧾

Keep it in the package

Carry your card and keep products in their original dispensary packaging.

🗺️

Don't cross state lines

Marijuana is still illegal under federal law, so it can't leave Florida with you, even to a legal state.
