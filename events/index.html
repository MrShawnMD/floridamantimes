Weird Florida Events

Frog legs, flying mullet, swamp cabbage, skunk apes and livestock. The oddball festivals, fairs and competitions coming up around Florida. Dates change, so check the official link before you go.

🐄Manatee County Fair

Palmetto · Jan 14–24, 2027

Eleven days of 4-H and FFA livestock barns, animal judging in the arena, a midway and fair food. Opening day is free. The cows have been preparing all year.

LaBelle · Feb 20–21, 2027

Deep-fried swamp cabbage fritters (that's hearts of palm, from the state tree) and actual armadillo races. Small-town Florida at full volume.

Sarasota · Mar 13–22, 2027

Ten days every March with livestock shows, the 4-H auction, rides and the famous elephant ear booth. Local kids, very large pigs.

Sopchoppy · Apr 10, 2027

Folks drive a stake into the ground and rub it until earthworms come wiggling up out of the dirt. Then they celebrate. Yes, really.

🐟Interstate Mullet Toss

Perdido Key (Flora-Bama) · Apr 23–25, 2027

Pay $20, grab a dead mullet, and hurl it from Florida across the state line into Alabama. Proceeds go to local charities. The fish did not consent.

Always the last full weekend of April; 2027 dates are estimated.
