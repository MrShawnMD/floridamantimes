How this works

A restaurant makes the Clean Plate Club when its most recent routine state inspection in the last 30 days found zero violations. It's a snapshot of one visit, not a guarantee, and plenty of great restaurants with a minor write-up won't appear. Featured spots are paid and always labeled.
