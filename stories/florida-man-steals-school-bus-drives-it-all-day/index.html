Florida Man Steals a School Bus, Drives It All Day Because He Figured He Could

No kids. No route. No plan. Just a big yellow dream and the open roads of Miami-Dade.

By the Florida Man Times Desk · Miami-Dade County · November 6, 2026

🚌

Some men buy a sports car when they need a little freedom. Others take up golf. One 23-year-old from Miami Gardens skipped both and allegedly went straight for a school bus.

According to the Miami-Dade Sheriff's Office, he slipped into the gated lot of a private bus company in the Gladeview area on a Wednesday and drove off in one of its buses. Not a van. Not a golf cart. A school bus. The vehicle you can spot from space, the one that comes with its own flashing stop sign.

And he didn't just take it around the block. Deputies say he drove it around for a whole day. Picture it: no students, no stops, no one fighting over the window seat. Just a man and a whole lot of yellow ambition, cruising.

When deputies caught up with him on Thursday and asked the obvious question, the arrest report says his answer was refreshingly honest. He did it because he thought he could get away with it. Bold strategy, for a getaway car built to haul a whole class and painted the brightest color known to science.

The plot twist: staff at the bus company told deputies they already knew him. Apparently this was not his first time taking something from their lot. At some point you stop being a thief and start being a regular.

He now faces three felony charges. As always, he is presumed innocent until proven guilty in court. The bus, we assume, is just glad to be home.
