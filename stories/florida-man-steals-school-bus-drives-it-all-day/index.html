Florida Man Steals a School Bus, Cruises Around All Day Because He Figured He Could

No kids, no route, no problem. Miami-Dade deputies say his reason was refreshingly honest.

By the Florida Man Times Desk · Miami-Dade County · November 6, 2026

🚌

Most people who wake up wanting a change of pace take a walk. One Miami Gardens man allegedly took a school bus.

According to the Miami-Dade Sheriff's Office, the 23-year-old slipped into the gated lot of a private bus company in the unincorporated Gladeview area and drove off in one of its buses. He then spent what deputies describe as a whole day behind the wheel, with no students and apparently no destination.

When deputies finally caught up with him and asked why, arrest documents say his answer was simple: he thought he could get away with it. Points for honesty, at least.

Here's the twist that makes it extra Florida. Staff at the bus company told deputies they already knew him, because this wasn't his first time taking something from them.

He now faces three felony charges. As always, an arrest is not a conviction, and he is presumed innocent unless proven otherwise in court.
