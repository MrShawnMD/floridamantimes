Florida Man Allegedly Pulls a Sword on His Roommate. The Issue? Sugar.

Lee County deputies say a coffee-sweetener dispute in North Fort Myers went from 'hey, that's mine' to medieval.

FM
Florida Man Times Desk

North Fort Myers · Nov 15, 2026 · 1 min read

Every roommate relationship has a breaking point. Dirty dishes. The thermostat. The guy who keeps 'borrowing' your shampoo. In one North Fort Myers home, deputies say the breaking point was sugar.

Here's how it went down, according to reports. On Sunday, November 15, a man finished making his morning coffee and confronted his roommate about using his sugar without asking. A fair complaint. A reasonable conversation. The kind that normally ends with a shrug and a trip to Publix.

Instead, Lee County deputies say the roommate answered with a martial arts weapon. Specifically, a sword. Over sugar. The stuff that costs a few bucks a bag.

Let's sit with that for a second. Somewhere in that house there was a calm, sensible response available. Maybe 'sorry, I'll replace it.' Maybe a strongly worded sticky note. The sword was not the calm, sensible response.

Deputies took the alleged swordsman into custody and booked him into the Lee County Jail on a charge of aggravated assault with a deadly weapon, which is a felony in Florida. Experts agree that sugar is not worth a felony. Neither is Splenda.

He is presumed innocent unless proven guilty. And to roommates everywhere: label your groceries, and maybe keep the décor weapons on the wall.
