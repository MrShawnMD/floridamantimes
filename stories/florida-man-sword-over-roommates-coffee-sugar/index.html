Florida Man Allegedly Pulls a Sword on His Roommate. The Issue? Sugar.

Lee County deputies say a coffee-sweetener dispute in North Fort Myers went from 'hey, that's mine' to medieval.

FM
Florida Man Times Desk

North Fort Myers · Nov 15, 2026 · 1 min read

The quick take

Lee County deputies say a North Fort Myers man grabbed a katana and held the blade inches from his roommate's face after the roommate complained about him using his sugar. Nobody was hurt; he's charged with aggravated assault with a deadly weapon.

Every roommate relationship has a breaking point. Dirty dishes. The thermostat. The guy who keeps borrowing your shampoo and swears he didn't.

In one North Fort Myers home, deputies say the breaking point was sugar. And the tiebreaker was a sword.

The morning brew

It happened on a Sunday in mid-November at a home off Harmony Avenue. According to the Lee County Sheriff's Office, one roommate had just finished making his coffee when he brought up a sore subject: the other roommate had been using his sugar without asking.

This is a reasonable thing to bring up. It is also, historically, how wars start.

Enter the katana

An argument followed. Deputies say the roommate accused of sugar theft then grabbed a katana, the long, curved Japanese sword you've seen in every samurai movie, walked up to the coffee drinker and held the blade just inches from his face while telling him, in so many words, to back off.

Nobody was hurt. The coffee's condition was not reported.

Let's pause on the equipment here. Most roommate disputes are settled with a passive-aggressive sticky note. Maybe a label maker. Florida, as always, chose violence, or at least deputies say one Floridian did.

Why a sword makes it a felony

Here's the part that's not a joke. In Florida, threatening someone with a deadly weapon counts as aggravated assault even if nobody gets touched. It's a third-degree felony, which can carry up to five years in prison. Waving a sword at your roommate over a packet of Domino is a very expensive way to win an argument.

What happens next

Deputies booked the man into the Lee County Jail on a charge of aggravated assault with a deadly weapon. The case is in its early stages and he is presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court.

Our advice for everyone else: buy your own sugar. Label it. And keep the cutlery that's longer than your arm in the closet.
