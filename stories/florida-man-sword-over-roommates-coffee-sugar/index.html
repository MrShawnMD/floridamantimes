Sugar Rush: Roommate Spat Over Coffee Sweetener Ends With a Sword and a Felony

Lee County deputies say a North Fort Myers argument about borrowed sugar got very sharp, very fast.

By the Florida Man Times Desk · North Fort Myers · November 15, 2026

☕

Every roommate has a line. For one North Fort Myers household, deputies say that line was the sugar.

According to reports, a man had just finished brewing his coffee when he confronted his roommate about using his sugar without asking. What should have ended with an apology and a trip to Publix instead escalated in a hurry.

Lee County deputies say the roommate answered the complaint by threatening him with a martial arts weapon: a sword.

The alleged sword-wielder was booked into the Lee County Jail on a charge of aggravated assault with a deadly weapon, a felony in Florida.

The lesson here: label your groceries. And maybe keep the décor weapons on the wall. The suspect is presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court.

Source: Tampa Free Press
