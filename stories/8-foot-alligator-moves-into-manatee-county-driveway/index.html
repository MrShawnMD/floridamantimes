Welcome to Florida: New Arrivals Find an 8-Foot Gator Parked in Their Driveway

The couple had just moved to Manatee County. The gator had apparently been there longer.

By the Florida Man Times Desk · Manatee County · September 2, 2026

🐊

Moving to Florida comes with a checklist: get a Florida license, find a good grouper sandwich, and learn what to do when an alligator moves in.

One couple who recently relocated to Manatee County got to skip straight to step three when they found an eight-foot alligator lounging in their driveway, living rent-free.

Rather than wander off, the big guy decided to stay put. Unsure what to do, the homeowners called police, who sent Florida Fish and Wildlife alligator trappers to handle the eviction.

The gator was removed. Officials didn't say where he was relocated. Hopefully somewhere with fewer cars and more fish.

If you find a gator that poses a threat to people or pets, call the FWC Nuisance Alligator Hotline at 866-392-4286. Don't try to move it yourself.

Source: FOX 35 Orlando
