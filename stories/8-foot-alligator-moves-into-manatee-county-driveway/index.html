Welcome to Florida: New Arrivals Find an 8-Foot Gator Parked in Their Driveway

The couple just moved to Manatee County. The gator was here first, and he'd like you to know it.

FM
Florida Man Times Desk

Manatee County · Sep 2, 2026 · 1 min read

Moving to Florida comes with a to-do list. Get a Florida driver's license. Learn what a Pub Sub is. Find out what your HOA thinks about your mailbox. For one couple who'd just moved to Manatee County, the list got a surprise item: evict the alligator.

They found an eight-foot gator lounging in their driveway, living rent-free like he'd signed the lease himself.

You'd think a giant reptile would take the hint and move along once humans showed up. Not this guy. He stayed put. No rush. No worries. Very Florida of him, honestly.

Not sure of the protocol when a dinosaur blocks your car, the homeowners called the police. Police sent Florida Fish and Wildlife alligator trappers, who are the people you want in this situation. Not your brother-in-law with a broom.

The trappers removed the gator. Officials didn't say where he was taken. We like to think somewhere with more fish, fewer minivans and no HOA.

Welcome to Florida, folks. And if a gator ever threatens people or pets at your place, don't play hero. Call the FWC Nuisance Alligator Hotline at 866-392-4286.
