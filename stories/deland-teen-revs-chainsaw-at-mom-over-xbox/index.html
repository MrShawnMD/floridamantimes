Florida Teen Allegedly Revs a Chainsaw at His Mom After She Takes Away His Xbox

Deputies say she filmed it. When she said she'd call the law, he said they wouldn't get there in time.

FM
Florida Man Times Desk

DeLand, Volusia County · Nov 28, 2026 · 2 min read

The quick take

Volusia Sheriff's Office deputies say a 16-year-old DeLand boy revved a chainsaw in his mother's face and threatened her after she took away his Xbox around 5 p.m. on Saturday, Nov. 21. The mother recorded video on her phone and fled outside to wait for deputies. No one was hurt, and the teen was charged with aggravated assault with a deadly weapon.

Every parent knows the moment. You take the game controller, the room goes quiet, and you brace for the slammed door. In DeLand, deputies say, the slammed door was skipped. The chainsaw was not.

According to the Volusia Sheriff's Office, a 16-year-old boy grabbed a chainsaw, fired it up and revved it in his mother's face after she took away his Xbox. Nobody was hurt. Everybody, presumably, needs a nap.

The argument

Deputies say the mother called 911 around 5 p.m. on Saturday to report a disturbance at a home on Lingering Lane, News 6 reported. Sources told the station the fight started when she confiscated his Xbox, and it also involved cosmetics the teen had hidden from her.

Things then went from grounded to horror movie. According to the arrest affidavit, as reported by News 6, the teen picked up a nearby chainsaw, turned it on and revved it in her face while threatening to kill her. News4JAX reported he also chased her and threatened to harm the family pets.

The video

The mother did what modern Floridians do in a crisis: she hit record. Deputies say her cellphone video shows the teen holding a green chainsaw and revving it again and again.

When she told him she was calling law enforcement, he answered, according to the affidavit: "They won't get here in time." It was meant as a threat. It plays more like a trailer for a movie nobody should make.

She left the trailer and waited outside for deputies, telling investigators she feared for her life.

What he told deputies

Deputies say the teen admitted he picked up the chainsaw to scare his mother, acknowledged chasing her and said he wanted to "beat" her. No injuries were reported.

Mock-serious public service announcement: screen time limits are not a matter for power tools. If you need to settle a dispute over a video game, the approved Florida methods are sulking, slamming a door, or negotiating for 15 more minutes like everybody else.

The charge

The teen was charged with one count of aggravated assault with a deadly weapon without intent to kill, a third-degree felony, according to News 6. For an adult, that kind of felony can carry up to five years in Florida prison, though cases involving minors usually go through the juvenile system. He was taken to the Volusia Sheriff's Office Family Resource Center.

Because he is a minor, his name has not been released, and we would not print it anyway. He is presumed innocent until proven guilty. If there is any justice, the chainsaw is grounded, too.
