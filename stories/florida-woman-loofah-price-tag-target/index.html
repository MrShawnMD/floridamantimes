Florida Woman Allegedly Rings Up Her Target Haul at Loofah Prices

Bedding, bath products and groceries, all priced like a bath sponge. Target staff were not fooled.

FM
Florida Man Times Desk

Florida · May 15, 2026 · 1 min read

Nobody walks out of Target spending only what they planned. It's the law of the red bullseye. You go in for toothpaste and come out with a throw pillow, a candle called 'Coastal Breeze' and a decorative bowl you will never use.

One Florida woman apparently found a workaround. According to reports, she used the price tag from a cheap loofah to ring up far pricier items. Bedding? Loofah price. Bath products? Loofah price. Groceries? You see where this is going.

As a concept, it's ambitious. If everything in the store costs the same as a loofah, Target becomes the Dollar Tree with better lighting.

The flaw in the plan was the people. A Target employee spotted what was going on and kept an eye on her as she loaded up her cart. Retail workers have seen it all, and they have absolutely seen the loofah trick.

Let's just say it did not end with a bargain. She is presumed innocent unless proven guilty. The loofah, for its part, never asked to be involved.
