Florida Man's Getaway Ends in a Porta-Potty, Minus His Pants, Police Say

A beach stroll, a stolen SUV, a smashed gate and the worst hiding spot on Sanibel Island.

FM
Florida Man Times Desk

Sanibel · Oct 27, 2025 · 1 min read

The quick take

Sanibel police say a 43-year-old man wandered off the beach, took a construction worker's SUV, nearly hit the worker, crashed through a gate and then hid in a porta-potty. He came out without his pants and was stopped with a Taser.

Sanibel Island is famous for seashells, sunsets and the Sanibel stoop, the bent-over shuffle of serious shell hunters.

On Oct. 27, 2025, police say one visitor added a new local attraction: the porta-potty standoff.

Act one: the beach

According to the arrest affidavit, a 43-year-old man walked up from the beach wearing only sweatpants and wandered onto a private property where construction work was going on. A worker told him to leave. He did not leave.

Instead, police say, he tried to break into a nearby shed and failed. Then he found the worker's Nissan SUV, unlocked, with the keys inside. Florida, please lock your cars.

Act two: the getaway

He allegedly drove off in the SUV and sped toward the worker, who had to jump out of the way. The worker wasn't hurt. The man then allegedly rammed through a gate, causing about $4,000 in damage, and parked the SUV on a different property.

His next move was, let's say, unconventional. He ran into a portable toilet and shut the door.

Act three: the porta-potty

When officers arrived, he came out of the porta-potty without his pants, carrying two wooden stakes, according to Gulf Coast News. As he headed toward another vehicle, an officer stopped him with a Taser.

Of all the hiding spots on a gorgeous barrier island, he chose the one place nobody wants to stay longer than necessary.

What happens next

He was charged with grand theft of a motor vehicle, aggravated assault with a deadly weapon and criminal mischief. He was released on Nov. 4, 2025, on a $7,500 bond, with an arraignment set for Dec. 8, 2025. As always, he is presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court.

The porta-potty has asked for privacy during this difficult time.
