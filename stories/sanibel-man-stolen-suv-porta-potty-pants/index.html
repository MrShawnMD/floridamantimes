Florida Man's Getaway Ends in a Porta-Potty, Minus His Pants, Police Say

A beach stroll, a stolen SUV, a smashed gate and the worst hiding spot on Sanibel Island.

By the Florida Man Times Desk · Sanibel · October 27, 2026

🚽

Sanibel Island is famous for seashells, sunsets and the 'Sanibel stoop,' the bent-over shuffle of serious shell hunters. On October 27, police say one visitor added a new local attraction: the porta-potty standoff.

According to the arrest affidavit, a 43-year-old man walked up off the beach wearing nothing but sweatpants. That's not a crime. In Florida it's barely a fashion statement. But then he wandered onto private property, and a construction worker told him to leave.

He did not leave. Police say he tried to break into a nearby shed instead. The shed won. So he moved on to the construction worker's Nissan SUV, which, in a decision the worker probably regrets, was unlocked with the keys inside.

The affidavit says he hopped in and gunned it toward the worker, who jumped out of the way and avoided injury. Then he allegedly rammed through a gate on his way out, causing about $4,000 in damage. Subtle.

Every great getaway needs a great hideout. This one picked a portable toilet. Police say that by the time it was over, he had shed his pants, which raises a lot of questions we have chosen not to ask.

He faces multiple charges and is presumed innocent until proven guilty. The porta-potty has asked for privacy during this difficult time.

Source: Tampa Free Press via AOL
