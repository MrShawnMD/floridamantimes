Beach Walk, Stolen SUV, Busted Gate, Porta-Potty: One Sanibel Man's Very Long Day

Police say the chase ended in the one place nobody wants to hide, and he left his pants behind.

By the Florida Man Times Desk · Sanibel · October 27, 2026

🚽

Sanibel is known for seashells and sunsets. On October 27, police say it was known for something else.

According to an arrest affidavit, a 43-year-old man walked up from the beach wearing nothing but sweatpants and wandered onto private property, where a construction worker told him to leave.

Instead, police say he tried to break into a shed. When that failed, he found the worker's SUV unlocked with the keys inside, hopped in and sped toward the worker, who jumped out of the way just in time.

He then allegedly smashed through a gate on his way out, causing about $4,000 in damage. The getaway didn't last. Police say he ended up holed up inside a portable toilet, where he apparently decided the sweatpants had to go too.

He faces multiple charges. As with every arrest, he is presumed innocent until proven guilty.
