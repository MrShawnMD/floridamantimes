Florida Man Shows Up to Polk County Booking With a Corkscrew Inside His Colon

He told deputies it had been in there for a year. A laxative did not change its mind.

FM
Florida Man Times Desk

Polk County · Nov 28, 2026 · 2 min read

The quick take

Polk County Sheriff Grady Judd said in a Friday morning video that a man arrived for booking at the county jail with a corkscrew inside his colon. According to WFLA, the man told deputies it had been there for a year and said he was not in pain. Deputies took him to a hospital, where a laxative did not work, and he was moved to the jail's medical dorm with the corkscrew still inside.

Florida has seen people try to sneak a lot of things into jail. Phones. Pills. In one famous Polk County case, an entire thermos. But this week's booking brought a new item to the inventory, and it came with no wine.

Sheriff Grady Judd says a man showed up to the Polk County Jail with a corkscrew inside his colon. Not in his pocket. Not in his shoe. In his colon. And, by his own account, it had been living there for about a year.

The booking

Judd shared the news in a social media video on Friday morning, Nov. 27, as part of his regular morning briefing, according to WFLA. The sheriff referred to the man only by his first name, and his full name and the charges that brought him to jail have not been released.

WFLA reports the man told deputies the corkscrew had been "in his system" for a year. He also told them he was not in pain, which is either a medical miracle or the most Florida sentence ever spoken.

How it got there, the reports do not say. That is probably for the best. Some mysteries are better left in the evidence room. Or, in this case, not in the evidence room.

The treatment plan

Deputies did what any reasonable person would do: they took him to the hospital. According to WFLA, the plan there was a laxative. The laxative, sadly, did not get the job done.

So deputies brought him back to the medical dorm at the jail. As of WFLA's report, the corkscrew was still inside him.

The sheriff, never one to skip a punchline, had a short summary of the situation: "We can't get it out; he didn't want it out." He also noted that the man did not even bring a bottle of wine, and joked about the corkscrew's route in, describing it as going north in the southbound lane.

Polk County's greatest hits

If this all sounds familiar, it is because Polk County has a track record. A few years back, Judd went viral describing a man who allegedly tried to smuggle a full-size thermos into the same jail by hiding it in his body. The sheriff called that hiding place the exit ramp, and the internet never forgot.

The corkscrew is smaller than a thermos. It is also pointier. Doctors everywhere would like you to know that neither one is a good idea, and that a corkscrew belongs in a kitchen drawer, next to the bottle opener and the takeout menus.

What happens next

The sheriff's office has not said whether surgery will be needed, and no new charges related to the corkscrew have been reported. The man's original charges have not been released either. Whatever brought him to jail, he is presumed innocent until proven guilty. The corkscrew, meanwhile, appears to be serving a life sentence.
