Florida Woman Dials 911 Three Times Because a Bar Cut Off Her Jell-O Shots

An Ocala bar said no. She said 'emergency.' Police said 'charges.'

By the Florida Man Times Desk · Ocala · June 2, 2026

🍸

Some emergencies are fires, crashes and medical crises. And then there's the Ocala bar that wouldn't serve one more Jell-O shot.

Police say a woman called 911 not once, not twice, but three times after a bartender refused to sell her the wobbly little drink.

Dispatchers, who presumably had real emergencies to handle, were not amused. Neither were officers, who charged her with misuse of the 911 system along with other offenses.

Friendly reminder from all of us at Florida Man Times: 911 is for emergencies. Being cut off at the bar is just a sign to call an Uber. She is presumed innocent unless proven guilty.
