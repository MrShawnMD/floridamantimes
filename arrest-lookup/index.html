Florida Arrest Lookup

Who got booked last night? Every link below goes to an official sheriff's office or state government search, so the information comes straight from the source and stays up to date. Florida Man Times doesn't host or copy booking records.

Look Up a Local Arrest

See who got booked, with photos, charges and bond, straight from each sheriff's own records. Most let you browse by booking date, so you don't need a name. An arrest or booking does not establish guilt.

  • Hillsborough CountyTampa · Brandon · Plant CityNo name needed: enter today's date in Booking Date, sort by Booking Date. (Brand-new arrests can take up to 24 hours to show.)HCSO Arrest Inquiry ↗
  • Polk CountyLakeland · Winter Haven · BartowNo name needed: tap the Booking Date tab, pick a day, hit Search.Polk Sheriff Jail Inquiry ↗
  • Pinellas CountySt. Pete · Clearwater · LargoNo name needed: search by Booking Date.Who's In Jail ↗
  • Lee CountyFort Myers · Cape Coral · Bonita SpringsNo name needed: set the Booking Date range and search.LCSO Arrest Search ↗
  • Sarasota CountySarasota · Venice · North PortSearch by date or name in the sheriff's search box.Arrests & Inmate Search ↗
  • Orange CountyOrlando · Winter Park · ApopkaReady-made list: tap Daily Booking List for everyone booked in the last 24 hours.Daily Booking List ↗
  • Broward CountyFort Lauderdale · Hollywood · PompanoReady-made list of recent bookings. No search needed.BSO Booking Blotter ↗
  • Every other Florida countyManatee, Charlotte, Pasco, Miami-Dade and moreLinks to every county jail's official search.Florida Dept. of State jail directory ↗

Booking information is an arrest record, not a conviction. For the final outcome of a case, check the county Clerk of Court.
