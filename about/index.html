About Florida Man Times

Florida is the only place where the news reads like fiction. Florida Man Times collects the strangest, funniest and most unbelievable true stories from across the Sunshine State and serves them up in one place.

Every story we publish starts with a real, published news report. We rewrite it in our own words and always link back to the original source so you can see it for yourself.

Some of our stories involve arrests. An arrest is only an accusation, and everyone is presumed innocent unless proven guilty in court. We cover the absurd, not the tragic.

Based in Sarasota, Florida. Of course.
