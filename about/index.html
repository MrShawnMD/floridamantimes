About Florida Man Times

Other states have news. Florida has stories. The kind where you read the headline, read it again, and then call someone just to say it out loud.

Florida Man Times is where those stories live. The stolen school buses. The gators who won't leave the driveway. The 911 calls that probably should have been texts to a friend.

Here's our deal. Every story starts with a real report from a real news outlet or police agency. We retell it in our own words, with jokes clearly labeled as jokes and facts kept straight. Every story links back to the original source so you can check our homework.

We laugh at bad decisions, never at people's suffering. And because an arrest is only an accusation, everyone in our arrest stories is presumed innocent unless a court says otherwise. Our story images are illustrations, not photos of the real people involved.

Based in Sarasota, Florida. Of course.
