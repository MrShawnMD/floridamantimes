How this works

Florida's Division of Hotels and Restaurants (DBPR) can order an emergency closure when an inspector finds an immediate health risk, most often roach or rodent activity. A closure is not a fine or a final judgment, and a restaurant has to pass a follow-up inspection before it reopens. Most reopen within a day. This list shows the state's records as published; conditions may have changed since.
