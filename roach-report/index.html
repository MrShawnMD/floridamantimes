How this works

Shut down means a state inspector found an immediate health risk, most often roaches or rodents, and ordered the restaurant closed until it passed a follow-up. Most reopen within a day.

High-priority violations are the most serious category on a Florida inspection (things like food held at unsafe temperatures, no handwashing, or pests). Fixed on the spot means the problem was corrected during the visit. A warning or recommended complaint is not a final judgment.
