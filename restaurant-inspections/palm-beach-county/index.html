    In the last 8 weeks the state ordered 14 Palm Beach County restaurants to close temporarily: Sal's Italian Ristorante in Palm Beach Gardens; El Sabor Latino Restaurante in Green Acres; Ihop 36-168 in Delray Beach; MacArthur's in Palm Beach Gardens; Fla Power & Light Cafeteria in Juno Beach; Pomona in Lake Worth Beach. Most reopen within a day once they pass a follow-up inspection.

    Where can I eat in Palm Beach County with a clean inspection?

    60 Palm Beach County restaurants passed their latest routine state inspection with zero violations in the last 30 days, including Paneterie (Delray Beach), Sabor Autentico Hondureno (Tamarac), The Olive Garden (Greenacres City), Jesi Caribbean Restaurant (Lantana), Piacere Fired Wood Pizza (Boca Raton), La Quinta Inn And Suites West Palm Beach Airport (West Palm Beach).

    How do I check a Palm Beach County restaurant's health inspection?
