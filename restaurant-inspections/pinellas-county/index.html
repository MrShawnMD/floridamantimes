❓ Pinellas County restaurant inspection questions

Which Pinellas County restaurants were shut down recently?

In the last 8 weeks the state ordered 18 Pinellas County restaurants to close temporarily: Halong Bay Vietnamese Cuisine in St Petersburg; Pofolks in St Petersburg; Siam Garden Thai Restaurant in St Petersburg; Mi Carreta Restaurant & Bakery in St Petersburg; Engine No 9 in St Petersburg; Chill Restaurant in St Petersburg. Most reopen within a day once they pass a follow-up inspection.

Where can I eat in Pinellas County with a clean inspection?

52 Pinellas County restaurants passed their latest routine state inspection with zero violations in the last 30 days, including Barefoot Beach House (Clearwater), Southern Fresh (Safety Harbor), Hot Tuna Sushi Bar And Grille (Oldsmar), Crispy Cones (Clearwater), Hampton Inn (Pinellas Park), The Melted State (Clearwater).

How do I check a Pinellas County restaurant's health inspection?

Florida's Division of Hotels and Restaurants (DBPR) inspects restaurants and posts every result online. This page pulls the state's records for Pinellas County every day, or you can search any restaurant on the DBPR website.
