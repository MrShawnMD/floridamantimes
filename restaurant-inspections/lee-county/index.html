    ❓ Lee County restaurant inspection questions

    Which Lee County restaurants were shut down recently?

    In the last 8 weeks the state ordered 8 Lee County restaurants to close temporarily: El Patio in Fort Myers; Le Goute in Fort Myers; Mix Latino in Fort Myers; Rib City in Estero; Los Taquitos Mexican Food Inc 3 in North Forth Myers; Huaraches Oaxaquenos in Fort Myers. Most reopen within a day once they pass a follow-up inspection.

    Where can I eat in Lee County with a clean inspection?

    46 Lee County restaurants passed their latest routine state inspection with zero violations in the last 30 days, including Holiday Inn Express Cape Coral (Cape Coral), The Summit Of Fort Myers (Fort Myers), A+gelato (Cape Coral), Big Nick's Barbecue (Cape Coral), Bella's Cakes (N Ft Myers), Brookes Nugget Wagon Inc (North Fort Myers).

    How do I check a Lee County restaurant's health inspection?

    Florida's Division of Hotels and Restaurants (DBPR) inspects restaurants and posts every result online. This page pulls the state's records for Lee County every day, or you can search any restaurant on the DBPR website.
