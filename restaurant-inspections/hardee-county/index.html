Strange · Ona

Solomon's Castle

A full-size castle in the middle of a Hardee County swamp, shiny with discarded aluminum printing plates and built over decades by artist Howard Solomon. There's a moat. There's a replica ship. There's a lot of puns. It's the most Florida thing Florida ever forgot to tell you about.

In rural Ona, Hardee County, about an hour east of Sarasota. Check hours and tour times before the drive.
