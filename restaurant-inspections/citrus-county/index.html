    ❓ Citrus County restaurant inspection questions

    Which Citrus County restaurants were shut down recently?

    In the last 8 weeks the state ordered 2 Citrus County restaurants to close temporarily: Mickeys Bar and Billards in Crystal River; Copp Winery and Brewery in Crystal River. Most reopen within a day once they pass a follow-up inspection.

    Where can I eat in Citrus County with a clean inspection?

    5 Citrus County restaurants passed their latest routine state inspection with zero violations in the last 30 days, including Breakfast Station (Crystal River), Robin's Country Kitchen (Floral City), Pizza Pi (Crystal River), Sundae Factory 3 (Homosassa), Chipotle Mexican Grill Of Lecanto (Lecanto).

    How do I check a Citrus County restaurant's health inspection?
