❓ Brevard County restaurant inspection questions

Which Brevard County restaurants were shut down recently?

In the last 8 weeks the state ordered 2 Brevard County restaurants to close temporarily: Broad St Diner in Titusville; Baci Beachside in Satellite Beach. Most reopen within a day once they pass a follow-up inspection.

Where can I eat in Brevard County with a clean inspection?

48 Brevard County restaurants passed their latest routine state inspection with zero violations in the last 30 days, including Morning Glory Eatery (Satellite Beach), Persis Indian Grill (Melbourne), Uncle Vito's Pizzeria (Melbourne), Salty Bagel And Grill (Melbourne), Soul Sushi 9 (Palm Bay), Domino's Pizza (Melbourne).

How do I check a Brevard County restaurant's health inspection?

Florida's Division of Hotels and Restaurants (DBPR) inspects restaurants and posts every result online. This page pulls the state's records for Brevard County every day, or you can search any restaurant on the DBPR website.
