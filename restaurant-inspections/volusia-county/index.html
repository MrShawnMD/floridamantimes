Cassadaga Spiritualist Camp

A tiny Volusia County town founded in the 1890s by Spiritualists, where many residents are mediums and the main business is talking to the dead. They call it the Psychic Capital of the World. The rest of us call it the only town where the receptionist might already know why you're there.

Southern Cassadaga Spiritualist Camp, off I-4 between Orlando and Daytona. Readings, a bookstore and walking tours; it's a residential community, so be respectful.

Which Volusia County restaurants were shut down recently?

In the last 8 weeks the state ordered 6 Volusia County restaurants to close temporarily: Daytona Taproom in Daytona Beach; Hard Deck Tiki Bar in Daytona Beach; Peppers Mexican Grill and Cantina in Ormond Beach; Parched Oak in Deland; Winghouse Bar + Grill in Daytona Beach; Mama Kitchen in Daytona Beach. Most reopen within a day once they pass a follow-up inspection.

Where can I eat in Volusia County with a clean inspection?

26 Volusia County restaurants passed their latest routine state inspection with zero violations in the last 30 days, including Ab Pho And Deli (Daytona Beach), Tailgatorz Sports Bar And Grill (Edgewater), Prestwick Golf Club (Ormond Bch), The Deland Stockyard (Deland), Perry's Ocean Edge Resort / Perry's Donut Lounge (Daytona Beach Shores), Dairy Queen Grill And Chill (Debary).

How do I check a Volusia County restaurant's health inspection?

Florida's Division of Hotels and Restaurants (DBPR) inspects restaurants and posts every result online. This page pulls the state's records for Volusia County every day, or you can search any restaurant on the DBPR website.
