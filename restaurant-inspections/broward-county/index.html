Where can I eat in Broward County with a clean inspection?

60 Broward County restaurants passed their latest routine state inspection with zero violations in the last 30 days, including Lola's Lola's On The Water (Pompano Beach), Bohemian Latin Grill (Oakland Park), Hollywoods Smoked Meat (Fort Lauderdale), Birria Bacana (Margate), The House Take Out (Lauderhill), Spice N Vybe (Oakland Park).
