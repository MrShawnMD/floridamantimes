❓ Orange County restaurant inspection questions

Which Orange County restaurants were shut down recently?

In the last 8 weeks the state ordered 12 Orange County restaurants to close temporarily: Winghouse Bar and Grill in Orlando; A Aki Sushi & Steakhouse in Orlando; Havana Bistro & Café in Orlando; Jcaribbean Cuisine LLC in Orlando; Antojitos Shecanita in Orlando; Teak Neighborhood Grill in Orlando. Most reopen within a day once they pass a follow-up inspection.

Where can I eat in Orange County with a clean inspection?

60 Orange County restaurants passed their latest routine state inspection with zero violations in the last 30 days, including Rosa Mexicano (Lake Buena Vista), Home 2 Suites - Flamingo Crossings (Winter Garden), Wyndham Lake Buena Vista - The Market (Lake Buena Vista), Stromboli's Ny Pizzeria (Windermere), A3 Confections (Orlando), F111 Fan Favorites (Orlando).

How do I check a Orange County restaurant's health inspection?

Florida's Division of Hotels and Restaurants (DBPR) inspects restaurants and posts every result online. This page pulls the state's records for Orange County every day, or you can search any restaurant on the DBPR website.
